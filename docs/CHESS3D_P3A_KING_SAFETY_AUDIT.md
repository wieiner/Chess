# Chess3D P3A King Safety Audit

P3A closes the draft Classic/Single-Side king-safety layer that P2O exposed as telemetry.

## Runtime Path

- Pseudo-legal movement remains in the existing 3D move generator.
- P3A adds a legal-filter layer instead of deleting the pseudo generator.
- Player-facing paths now use legal moves for Classic and Single-Side:
  - legal action preview;
  - `TryMakeMove`;
  - side legal-action counts;
  - game outcome checks;
  - action perft/divide diagnostics.

## Piece And Board Assumptions

- Board: 8x8x8, 512 projected integer cells.
- Piece code: `side * 10 + pieceType`.
- King piece type: `6`.
- Sides use the existing side ids and face-forward vectors from the active RuleProfile.
- CoreCell stacks are not part of Classic/Single-Side king-safety truth because those profiles use exclusive occupancy.

## Attack Geometry

The attack kernel follows existing 3D movement definitions:

- pawns attack one forward step plus one perpendicular offset;
- knights use the existing 3D L-move set;
- rooks attack along orthogonal axes;
- bishops/officers attack along diagonal directions generated by the engine;
- queens combine rook and bishop/officer line attacks;
- kings attack adjacent one-step cells.

Sliding attacks stop at the first occupied cell. Capturing the checking piece or blocking a sliding line is legal when the resulting position leaves the moving side's king safe.

## Profile Boundary

- Classic Six-Side: real runtime check, checkmate, and stalemate.
- Single-Side Training: uses the same king-safety filter when a king is present, while remaining a training/sandbox profile.
- Asgard/Rubik/Hodge: do not inherit Classic checkmate outcomes. Their victory and action semantics remain profile-specific.

## Safe Change Shape

The old exported ABI remains intact. P3A changes internal truth behind existing getters and summaries, plus contract tests and regression fixtures.
