# Chess3D Camera And Readability

P3C adds runtime-only camera and readability controls.

## Camera Presets

- Isometric.
- Top.
- Side.
- Reset.
- Existing orbit, pan, lift, and mouse wheel zoom remain available.

## Backgrounds

- Neutral is the default.
- Light helps screenshots and manual QA.
- Dark remains optional, not the default.

## High Contrast

The high-contrast toggle uses readable fallback piece materials. This helps diagnose texture/material problems and prevents black pieces from disappearing into the scene.

These options do not change engine state or save/replay data.
